FAQ

Questions to settle before checkout.

Short answers for Pakistan-based buyers comparing a low headline price with a workable operating cost.

Are these live vendor quotes?

No. These are dated public pricing inputs. Confirm term, taxes, region, add-ons and the final checkout total with the vendor.

Why compare renewal cost?

Introductory rates and long prepayment terms can hide the second-year bill. The calculator keeps first-year and renewal-aware totals visible together.

Is the PKR amount a live exchange quote?

No. PKR is calculated from the rate you enter, so you can use your bank, card or accounting rate.

Will a referral link change my price?

Current links are direct official links because affiliate IDs are blank. Any later approved referral relationship remains disclosed and does not add a markup.
